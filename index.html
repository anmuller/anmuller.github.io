Antoine Muller


I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanics at Claude Bernard University Lyon 1. I conduct my research at the Laboratory of Biomechanics and Impact Mechanics (LBMC, UMR T_9406). My work mainly focuses on developing numerical tools for in situ motion analysis. Currently, my research is centered on markerless video-based motion analysis.

For any inquiries, feel free to reach out to me via mail!
